Inju Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

  • Inju has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run any servers.
  • The stamps, signatures, photos, and PDFs you create, and anything you type into forms, are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.
  • To keep the app free, it shows Google AdMob ads. To serve those ads, Google processes device information such as your advertising ID.
  • To fix and improve the app, it collects usage statistics through Google Firebase Analytics, such as which screens were opened and whether an ad was watched to the end. Names, signatures, and document contents are not sent.

Sonteve (the "developer"), the individual developer who made Inju (the "app"), handles users' personal information with care and complies with applicable laws, including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The app is available in Korean and English; the Korean version of this policy is here.

1. Personal information collected by the developer

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, or national ID number. The app has no sign-up or login, and the developer does not run any servers. However, the app usage statistics described in Section 5 are collected through Google Firebase, and the developer views these statistics in the Firebase console.

2. Information processed only on your device

The information below is created and stored only on your device so that the app's features can work. It is not sent to the developer or any third party.

InformationStamp and signature images, photos of physical stamps, PDF files you open, PDFs you have stamped or signed, and the names, contact details, amounts, and other details you enter into forms (IOU, power of attorney, letter of pledge)
Where it is storedThe app's private storage. Stamp images and stamped PDFs are also saved to your phone's shared folders (Pictures > Inju and Download > Inju when the app is in English, or the folders named "인주" when it is in Korean). Signature images are saved to your Pictures folder only if you choose to save them to your photos.
When it leaves your deviceOnly when you tap Share and pick another app yourself, such as KakaoTalk or email. It is then passed only to the app you picked.

3. Permissions the app requests

CameraUsed only to photograph a physical stamp on paper and remove the background. The photo is processed only on your device. (Optional)

When you choose a photo, the app uses your phone's photo picker, so it does not request permission to access your photos; only the one photo you pick is passed to the app. Likewise, when you open a PDF, only the one file you pick is passed to the app. Saving to the shared folders does not require any extra permission.

You can use every other feature without allowing camera access. You can turn off a permission you have granted at any time in your phone's Settings > Apps > Inju > Permissions.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free. Before some features (saving a stamp, saving a stamped PDF, creating a form PDF, and saving a physical stamp image), it shows a rewarded ad that you choose to watch. Ads are provided by Google LLC's AdMob service.

  • To show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and information about your interactions with ads while using the app.
  • Google processes this information directly. The developer does not receive information that can identify individual users and sees only aggregate statistics such as ad revenue and impressions.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy
  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form when they first open the app and are asked whether they consent to ad-related data use. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy settings at the bottom of the app's home screen.
  • If you do not want personalized ads, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads.

5. App usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google LLC's Firebase Analytics to understand how it is used and to fix problems.

Information collectedScreens opened, time spent using the app, ad results (watched to the end, closed partway, canceled, or no ad available) and the name of the related feature, the app instance ID (a random number generated by Firebase), advertising ID, device model, operating system, app version, and approximate location (country and city level, estimated from your IP address)
Information not collectedAnything you enter or create, such as stamp and signature images, photos, PDFs, and the names, contact details, and amounts you type into forms
Retention periodKept for up to 14 months under the Google Analytics data retention setting, then deleted.

Learn more: Privacy and Security in Firebase, Google Privacy Policy

6. Sharing with third parties and outsourced processing

The developer does not provide users' personal information to third parties. The following work is entrusted to a service provider to run the app.

Google LLCAd delivery (AdMob, Section 4) and app usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, Section 5). The information is sent to Google's servers, including in the United States, while you use the app, and is processed under Google's policies.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Stamps, signatures, PDFs, and settings stored inside the app are deleted when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. The app does not use Google's automatic backup.
  • Files saved to the shared folders (Pictures > Inju or 인주, and Download > Inju or 인주) are your own files, so they remain after you uninstall the app. You can delete them yourself.
  • No information is kept on the developer's servers. App usage statistics follow the retention period in Section 5.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 14, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

The developer does not keep personal information that can identify you. You can manage the information on your device yourself through the app or your phone's settings. App usage statistics will no longer be linked to the same device once you delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Ads or uninstall the app. If you would like your usage statistics deleted, or if you have any questions, please contact us below.
